Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy. You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app, or contact us.

How to delete your data from the app:

  1. Open EzyCooking and go to Settings
  2. Tap Manage Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion — all your data (recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and account info) will be permanently removed

If you can't access the app or need help, email us at mohitpachisia1@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we'll process it within 7 days.
